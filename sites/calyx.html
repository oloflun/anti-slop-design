“A bouquet is a conversation between three flowers and the silence between them.”

Calyx is a two-sister atelier in Vasastan, working only with what is cut that morning at the market hall in Stockholm and pulled from our growing partners in Småland and Skåne. We accept eleven commissions a week. The eleventh is a wedding.

a thing

We accept private and trade commissions on Mondays only. You write us about the room, the light, the meal, the person. We write back about what is cuttable that week. If a fit exists, we make a proposal in a single email. No portal, no calendar link.
