# Klova Hamnkrog — the warm-photographic register

Source extracted from `~/klova-hamnkrog/src/app/` (Next.js + Tailwind 4). Kept
here because the other worked examples in `sites/` are all editorial print, and a
model that only reads those converges on serif, cream, and roman numerals for
every business it is given.

**This page is right, and it breaks most of the editorial invariants.** That is
the point. A harbour restaurant in the archipelago wants to feel like a warm
Saturday, not like a limited edition.

What it does instead:

- **Centred logo lockup over a full-bleed photograph.** No label rail, no kicker,
  no edition marker. The photograph and the mark carry the fold.
- **A real button.** `SE VÅR MENY`, a plain outlined rectangle, sized to be
  pressed. In this register a typeset mailto at text-4xl would be affectation:
  people arriving here want to press something.
- **Rounded corners and generous air around images**, rather than full-bleed with
  type over. Friendly, not cinematic.
- **A carousel with dots** for the food gallery — a pattern the editorial
  registers would reject, correct here because the content is genuinely a set of
  equivalent images and the audience expects to flip through them.
- **No grain film, no apparatus, no colophon, no ledger.** The menu is a menu.
- **Warm serif display over a plain sans body**, at conversational sizes. The
  headline welcomes; it does not announce.

What it still shares with every register: one accent, hierarchy through scale
rather than decoration, a tonal inversion at the footer, photography that was
actually art-directed, and copy in a specific voice about a specific place.

Read this one when the business is hospitality, tourism, a guesthouse, a family
firm, or anything whose promise is warmth and welcome rather than precision or
connoisseurship.
