Systems with warmth. 温度のある機械

For three days in mid-September we close the Studieshus in central Lund and gather forty builders, researchers, designers and writers who all hold the same suspicion: that a machine can be useful without being cold, and that scale does not have to mean indifference.

Programme

Three days. One question.

Day 01
12.ix · friday

Drawing the future

未来を描く

Talks on what we are building toward, not just away from. Material talks from Watanabe (Tokyo), Brännström (Stockholm) and Asker (Reykjavík). The afternoon is a single long workshop on speculative scenario writing.

  • 09:00Doors · Coffee & cardamom rolls
  • 10:30Watanabe — Soft Infrastructures
  • 13:00Lunch · long-table
  • 14:30Workshop · Brännström
  • 19:00Dinner at Bantorget 9
Day 02
13.ix · saturday

Creating together

共に創る

The whole day is collaborative — six teams of seven, each given the same brief at sunrise and a working room until ten. By evening we present and argue. Auböck and Lindqvist sit on the wall. No slides, only material.

  • 08:00Brief at sunrise · Stadsparken
  • 09:30Teams form · rooms assigned
  • 12:00Working · lunch in studio
  • 19:00Presentations · the wall
  • 22:00Open evening · Akademiska Föreningen
Day 03
14.ix · sunday

A long, slow walk back

ゆっくり歩いて帰る

Sunday is unscheduled. We commission six writers to publish 800-word essays the same week. Some attendees walk in pairs to Bjärred and back along the coast. The bus to Copenhagen Kastrup leaves at half-past four; the conversations don’t.

  • 09:00Brunch · open atrium
  • 11:00Three closing readings
  • 13:00Walks to Bjärred · sjöbod-coffee
  • 16:30Bus departs · Kastrup
Speakers — Edition 03

Eight of forty,
on the wall.

Kenji Watanabe

Machine-learning artist · Tokyo

Co-builder of Soft Infrastructures — generative city studies commissioned by Tokyo Metropolitan Government.

Astrid Vinge

Neural-interface researcher · Lund

Author of Hand-Held Cognition (2024). Studies attention as a designable material.

Margot Lindqvist

Architect · Stockholm

Designer of Den Långa Korridoren, a 1.2km library in Umeå. Writes about soft buildings.

Ravi Auböck

Industrial designer · Vienna

Co-founder of Atelier Lichtspur. Designs objects so they age, not break.

Sofia Brännström

Computational poet · Göteborg

Workshops at Hyperborea since 2024. Authored A Grammar for Machines That Apologise.

Thomas Asker

Geothermal engineer · Reykjavík

Leads the Hellisheiði heat-capture project. Talks about underground infrastructure as a polite material.

Yuki Kosugi

Ceramicist & coder · Kyoto

Trains diffusion models on her own glaze tests. Spoke first at Edition 01.

Lukas Helle

Astrophysicist · Aarhus

Spends six weeks a year at La Silla. Reads aloud at Hyperborea every year. Refuses slides.

Installation 03

Sundial,
for forty
conversations.

A site-specific installation by Margot Lindqvist and Yuki Kosugi: forty hand-thrown ceramic bowls, arranged in a fourteen-meter sundial in the central atrium. Each attendee places a single object in their bowl on Friday. We photograph the sundial at the same hour every day. On Sunday morning the bowls are mailed home.
