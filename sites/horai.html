THE
HOUSE

est. mmxxiv

Hōrai is a single dining room on the top floor of a former bookbinder's workshop on Hornsgatan. The chefs are three sisters — Ines, Mai and Linnea Bergström, born in Stockholm, raised between Kyoto and Bangkok — cooking what each thinks the others meant. The menu changes weekly. There is no à la carte. The wine list is short on purpose; the sake list is not.

Named for the immortal mountain in the eastern sea — a place no one has ever quite been to, only described.

部屋

THE ROOM

The dining room is built from raw ash and oxidised brass, twenty-six covers around a single kitchen pass. Lacquer-red walls, dim and warm, with a single window that looks across the Riddarfjärden to Gamla Stan. The architect was Olof Almgren; the lighting is by Hanna Rune.

The room is loud at the start of the evening and quiet by the end. We close the door at half past six.
