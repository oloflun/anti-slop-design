## Why Kvist
- No more flaky integration tests. Record real traffic instead of maintaining hand-written mocks.
- Reproduce a bug by replaying the exact request sequence that caused it, instead of guessing at repro steps.
- Recordings stay on your machine, so you're not routing traffic through a third-party server to test locally.

## Record · Replay · Diff
**Record.** Kvist captures every request and response your app makes: headers, bodies, and timing.
**Replay.** Run the same sequence again, against a new build, a different branch, or another environment.
**Diff.** When a response changes, Kvist shows you exactly what's different, instead of leaving you to compare payloads by hand.

2. Vi tar fram ett förslag utifrån det.
3. På bröllopsdagen levererar och sätter vi upp allt.
