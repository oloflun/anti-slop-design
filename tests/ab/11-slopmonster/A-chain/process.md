# Process — 11-slopmonster / A-chain

Three passes applied in order to `draft.md`, per the copy-gate: copywriting (structure/persuasion) → copy-editing (seven sweeps) → humanizer / humanizer-svenska (AI-tell removal). Honest-copy discipline from `copy-gate.md` (never invent a metric, customer, or quote) was enforced across all three passes, not just the last.

## Pass 1 — copywriting

- Rewrote both heroes around a single concrete value prop instead of a hype claim. Kvist: "Unlock seamless API confidence… game-changing developer experience" became "Know what your API actually changed" — specific, not generic SaaS praise. Florhuset: "Blommor som berör… en upplevelse" became "Handbundna buketter, mitt i Majorna" — locality is the actual differentiator, not an abstract feeling.
- Corrected the audience mismatch in Kvist's hero: draft addressed "solo hacker or fast-growing team," but the brief's audience is small teams without QA. Dropped the mismatched line; the Solo/Team split in Pricing already carries that distinction.
- Killed the two unsupported claims: "Trusted by 10,000+ happy developers worldwide" and "populära ateljékvällar." Per copy-gate's honest-copy rule, neither got a fabricated number or a placeholder — each was replaced with a true, verifiable benefit (local-only recordings; a plain description of the class) rather than an invented stat.
- Upgraded weak CTAs: "Get started for free" and "Join thousands of developers who've made the switch" both leaned on vague or unproven claims. Replaced with "Start recording free" (repeated at hero and closing CTA) and a direct "Stop guessing what broke your API."
- Left all given numbers untouched (495/425/345/295 kr, 190 kr/seat, 490 kr, opening hours, Mariaplan 4) — no invented figures anywhere, so no `[needs number]` placeholders were needed.

## Pass 2 — copy-editing (seven sweeps)

- Clarity/Specificity: cut vague abstractions ("elevate your workflow," "en upplevelse," "det som verkligen betyder något") in favor of concrete actions (record/replay/diff a request, hand-tie a bouquet, deliver on the wedding day).
- So What: bouquet descriptions moved from decorative metaphor ("en explosion av färg," "en harmonisk symfoni," "elegans möter naturlighet") to what the reader actually gets — season, color pairing (kept only where the draft itself stated the colors), or restraint — without adding new invented detail.
- Zero Risk: hero and CTA now state plainly what happens on click ("Start recording free," "Beställ bukett") instead of vague momentum language ("Ready to elevate your workflow?").
- Sentence-level tightening throughout: shorter sentences in the wedding steps and the Ateljékvällar paragraph, one idea per line, no sentence over ~25 words.
- Bullet formatting standardized to a label + colon instead of the draft's inline em dash, for scannability and consistency across both pages' lists.

## Pass 3 — humanizer / humanizer-svenska

- Removed every em dash (—) and en dash used as a clause connector on both pages; kept en dashes only where they mark a genuine numeric/time range (18–20.30, tisdag–fredag), which is ordinary Swedish typographic convention, not the clausal-connector tell the rule targets.
- Cut promotional/cliché language flagged by name in the skill: "in the heart of" → "Belägen i hjärtat av Majorna" became a plain "ligger på Mariaplan 4 i Majorna"; "nestled," "seamless," "game-changing," "leverage," "empowers" all removed from the English page.
- Removed negative parallelism and aphorism formulas: "not just a proxy, it's a game-changing experience," "inte bara en blomsterbutik — det är en upplevelse," "elegans möter naturlighet" (X möter Y formula).
- Removed forced rule-of-three padding: "effortlessly, reliably, and locally," "passion, precision och kärlek till hantverket," "skräddarsytt förslag som speglar er unika stil."
- Fixed tailing negations and empty filler: "It's that simple," "so you're not guessing, you're knowing," "utan att du behöva lyfta ett finger," "sömlöst och stressfritt" — replaced with a real clause stating what actually happens.
- Cut the generic warm-up sentence before the wedding section ("Din stora dag förtjänar det allra bästa") per the fragmented-header pattern — the section now opens straight on the concrete offer.
