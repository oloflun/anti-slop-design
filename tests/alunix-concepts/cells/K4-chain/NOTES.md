# K4 — Journal. Notes.

## Reference lock

Primary reference/direction: none of the three is primary. Principle only, borrowed in
equal small amounts, never structure/palette/type: **Calyx** (plates beside text, kickers
as record, one accent doing one job), **Raus** (whisper-weight 300 as the load-bearing
voice, hairlines instead of shadow, single-accent role discipline), **Redbrick Coffee**
(running-head-plus-hairline section openers, ink accent that never fills a surface).

Preserve (principle, 3-5): kicker-as-record in mono, capped small, never larger than a
caption; plates captioned like an archive, never a bare image; exactly one accent with one
named job; hairlines and ton-sur-ton for depth, zero shadow, zero card; serif carries
voice, mono carries register, the two never swap jobs.

Borrow only (1-2): the `NN / total` plate counter (Calyx's signature move, assigned to K4
by the diversity contract); the running-head-plus-rule chapter opener (Redbrick).

Rejected: Calyx's cream + rust + Cardo + PT Mono system in full — K4 has to look nothing
like it, since Calyx is one of the three demo screenshots shown ON this page; Raus's
marigold/pine-green role split and its 20-40px radius softness; Redbrick's scarlet ink and
Editorial Old display serif.

Media strategy: the three demo screenshots as evidence plates, vendored from
`~/alunix/public/demos/`; one atmosphere photo (`k4-cabin-desk.webp`, Unsplash, credited
in the colophon) beside the hero headline, never presented as Alunix's real office. No
stock people, no logos, no invented testimonials.

## Decision ledger

**Tier:** bound by contract — K4's row in `contract.md` fixes authority, category,
register, grammar, world and fingerprint; `TASTE.md` §§1/4/6 set the harmony rules; the
catalogue lock supplies principle only. Nothing here is free invention.

**Register:** editorial print. **Grammar:** chaptered editorial (contract-assigned;
already distinct from Calyx's own gallery/catalog grammar).

**World preamble** ("Varm tryckt journal"): the surface (paper) and the plates carry the
page. Departure from the forbidden AI-look #1 (cream + terracotta + serif-italic
two-tone) on two axes, as the contract requires:

1. **Palette temperature** — cold stone-grey paper `#E7E9E3`, not warm cream. Ink-violet
   accent `#332B4D`, not terracotta/rust/scarlet, and not K1's or K3's blue, not Raus's
   pine green.
2. **Type pairing** — Fraunces (serif, weight 300, roman only, no italic anywhere on the
   page) paired with IBM Plex Mono for every label/counter/kicker. No roman+italic
   two-tone headline.

Grammar also differs from Calyx's own (gallery/catalog vs chaptered editorial) — a third
point of separation beyond the two the contract requires.

Accent's one job: numerals only — the three chapter numerals, the three `NN / 03`

## Feeling curve

1. Recognition — "samma händer" headline, the plate beside it a real desk, not a stock
   office
2. Trust — three real, clickable demo sites, each visibly different from the others and
   from this page
3. Understanding — the four-step ledger, honest placeholders instead of invented numbers
4. Resolve — the colophon: one person, one mailbox, printed like the back page of a real
   journal

No scrub, no pin, no spotlight, no magnet — banned outright by the chaptered-editorial
grammar. The page's only "set piece" is the counter/numeral system, which is content, not
decoration.
