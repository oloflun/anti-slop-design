Invarianter (ordagrant, bryt dem aldrig):
- Motion always plays (F-011). prefers-reduced-motion is deliberately not honoured on this project. Suggesting a reduced-motion fallback, a static alternative, or a @media (prefers-reduced-motion) block is out of scope.
- The scroll engine is never edited. scroll/scrollcraft.js and scrollcraft.css are vendored; sc-smooth.js is the sanctioned shim.
- --sc-p is the raw scroll position; --sc-ps is the smoothed one. Anything that physically moves must read --sc-ps.
- No em-dashes in visible text (gate 75).
- sites/* rörs aldrig (Hōrai, Calyx, Hyperborea är ramade exempelsajter). Copyn är låst (copy-gaten körd), döm inte ordval. Palett och typsnitt väljs inte om.
- Review only, no patches.
