## Why it works

The product screenshot — a support inbox with ticket threads and AI-drafted
replies — doesn't float on a flat colour or a device frame; it sits on a soft,
out-of-focus floral gradient photograph, pink blurring into teal. That single
choice softens what would otherwise be a fairly technical, dense UI screenshot,
giving an "AI support copilot" product a calmer, warmer visual register than
the category default (dark, technical, terminal-styled dashboards).

The same gradient-photo backdrop reappears behind all three feature cards
further down the page ("Resolve in seconds," "Learn from your docs," "Know
when to escalate"), each showing a different, smaller UI moment on the
identical soft background. Reusing one backdrop image across four separate
screenshots ties the whole page together visually without needing four
different photographs — one asset, repeated with different UI crops, reads as
a system rather than a scattered set of illustrations.

A small "NEW — Auto-resolution is now live" pill sits above the headline,
doing lightweight, low-commitment news signalling before the reader has
committed to reading the full pitch — a habit borrowed from changelog-driven
products.

## What to borrow (principle)

- **Set product screenshots against a soft, out-of-focus photographic
  gradient** rather than a flat colour or device frame, to warm up an
  otherwise technical UI.
- **Reuse one backdrop image behind multiple feature screenshots** so a set of
  otherwise-unrelated UI crops reads as one consistent system.
- **Post a small "what's new" pill above the headline** for returning visitors,
  without dedicating a whole section to it.
- **Keep the palette almost entirely photographic** (the gradient backdrop) plus
  cream and black — no separate brand accent colour needed.

## What to leave

- A soft floral/watery gradient backdrop, while calming, is a fairly common
  choice across current AI-product templates — it risks reading as a category
  default rather than a considered brand choice unless paired with a specific,
  ownable colour combination.
- Repeating the identical backdrop image behind four different screenshots can
  flatten the sense of product depth if the crops are too similar in
  composition — each card needs a genuinely distinct UI moment to earn the
  repetition.

## Signature move

The single reused gradient backdrop: one soft, out-of-focus floral-to-teal
photograph appears behind the hero screenshot and all three feature-card
screenshots, unifying four different UI crops into one visual system with a
single image asset.

## Brief

Build a calm, cream-and-photographic AI-product page. Choose one soft,
out-of-focus gradient photograph (floral, watery, or abstract) as a single
recurring backdrop asset. Open with a small "what's new" pill above a plain
headline, with the primary product screenshot floating on the gradient
backdrop beside the copy. Run a partner/integration logo row beneath. Build a
three-card feature grid where each card reuses the same backdrop image behind
a distinct, smaller UI screenshot. Keep the palette to cream, near-black and
whatever colours live inside the one photographic backdrop — no separate brand
accent.
