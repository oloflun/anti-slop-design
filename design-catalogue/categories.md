# Categories

Start set, 2026-09-04. One primary per entry, optional `category2`. Revised after the read-through
that writes `TASTE.md`; `index.py` reads the bold name and the description from this list.

- **playful** — colour, motion or illustration carries the mood; the page is allowed to be fun before it is efficient (consumer brands, apps for people, snacks, events).
- **institutional** — authority through calm: funds, firms, agencies with a mandate, public bodies; restraint, hierarchy, few devices, long-lived.
- **portfolio** — a person or studio showing work; the work is the content, the page is the frame; name, selected projects, contact.
- **product-focused** — software or a tool sold on what it does; product surface as hero, feature choreography, pricing, proof by demo.
- **editorial** — journals, magazines, publishers, brands that behave like print: columns, plates, kickers, chapters, reading rhythm.
- **hospitality** — hotels, restaurants, cabins, destinations, retreats; place and atmosphere first, booking as the single action.
- **consumer-brand** — D2C objects and drinks; packshots, colour worlds, campaigns, shop.
- **luxury-craft** — materials, provenance, restraint at a high price; slow pages, fewer words, texture over colour.
